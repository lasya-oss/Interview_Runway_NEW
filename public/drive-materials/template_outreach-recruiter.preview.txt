## Give a recruiter a clear fit signal

Worked example - fictional. Names, organizations, dates, amounts, metrics, and outcomes below are invented to demonstrate the method. Do not copy them as your own experience. Use only facts you can explain and support.

Learning objective: Write a short first message that identifies the role, supports relevance with evidence, and asks for one easy next step.

## Use role, evidence, ask

The recipient needs to know which opening you mean and why your background is relevant. Choose one result that maps to a major requirement. Avoid listing every technology, describing your entire career, or claiming to be a perfect fit. Check that the opening is current before contacting someone.

## Worked message - fictional

Hi Riya, I am a backend engineer with four years of experience building account and notification services. I recently improved delivery recovery by introducing idempotent requests and failure-injection tests. I am interested in OrbitDesk's backend reliability opening because its service-ownership and distributed-systems responsibilities match that work. If you support this opening, would you be open to a brief conversation? My resume is attached. Thank you, Nila.

## Check the message

The example names a role family, gives one supported engineering signal, connects it to the opening, and ends with one question. In your version, include only a resume or portfolio link that works for the recipient. Avoid attachments or sensitive information the company has not requested.

If the recipient is not the right contact, a polite redirection is enough. Follow any stated application process; outreach does not replace it. Record whom you contacted so you do not send duplicate messages through several channels.
