Timeline Extension Request
Decision-extension request that gives a concrete reason and a bounded date.
How to use: Replace bracketed fields, delete instructions that do not apply, and keep every claim truthful and interview-defensible.
Email template
Subject: Request to extend offer decision to [date]
Hi [Name],
Thank you again for the offer. I am completing [specific diligence / previously scheduled process / family or relocation review] and want to make a considered commitment.
Would it be possible to extend the decision deadline from [current date] to [requested date]? I will provide a firm answer by then and will let you know sooner if I can.
I remain enthusiastic about [specific role reason].
Best, [Name]
Use well
Ask early
Request the shortest useful extension
Give a truthful, concise reason
Do not imply acceptance before deciding
