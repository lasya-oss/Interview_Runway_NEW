## Lead with the team's engineering problem

Worked example - fictional. Names, organizations, dates, amounts, metrics, and outcomes below are invented to demonstrate the method. Do not copy them as your own experience. Use only facts you can explain and support.

Learning objective: Show a hiring manager a concrete connection between the team's needs and your work without prescribing an architecture from outside the company.

## Research only enough to be specific

Use the role description or a public engineering discussion to identify a real problem. Treat your interpretation as an inference, not inside knowledge. Then offer one relevant experience. The goal is to make a conversation useful, not to audit the team's architecture in a cold message.

## Worked message - fictional

Hi Arun, I saw the backend opening on OrbitDesk's notification team. The role description's emphasis on recovery after provider failures stood out because I recently owned a similar reliability improvement. I introduced stable request keys, bounded retries, and a reconciliation report after duplicate sends were observed, then tested the recovery path under injected timeouts. I would be glad to discuss how that experience maps to your team's priorities. Would a short conversation be useful? My resume and a sanitized design note are included. Best, Nila.

## Avoid the common failure

Do not tell the manager to adopt a technology before understanding the requirements. Do not imply that public symptoms prove an internal defect. Keep proprietary details out of examples and label a demonstration project accurately. Use a clear, low-pressure request and follow the normal application process.
