## Use real alternatives without turning them into threats

Worked example - fictional. Names, organizations, dates, amounts, metrics, and outcomes below are invented to demonstrate the method. Do not copy them as your own experience. Use only facts you can explain and support.

Learning objective: Communicate a genuine competing deadline and one comparable package fact while protecting confidentiality and maintaining a credible decision process.

## Compare before mentioning

Verify that the alternative is real and understand its currency, fixed versus variable components, engagement model, operating expectations, and deadline. A larger headline is not automatically a better package. Only disclose information you are permitted and comfortable to share. The template is a communication aid, not a market-price or legal determination.

## Worked message - fictional

Subject: Offer timing and package discussion

Hi Riya, I want to be transparent that I have another written offer with a decision date of 18 September. Your role remains compelling because of the end-to-end service ownership and the team's reliability priorities. After comparing the same annual fixed-cash component under the stated engagement models, the other package is stronger on fixed compensation. Is there flexibility to improve that component? I would appreciate knowing what is possible by 16 September so I can make a considered decision. Best, Nila.

## Boundaries that preserve credibility

The message gives a real deadline, identifies the component, and asks a question without demanding a match or promising acceptance. If you choose to name an amount, state the currency and time unit and distinguish guaranteed from conditional value. Never forward a confidential offer letter simply because someone asks; consider what can be summarized instead.

Decide in advance what you will do if the package cannot change. Thank the team for a clear answer, compare the full operating reality, and communicate your final decision promptly. Never fabricate an offer, deadline, or willingness to accept.
