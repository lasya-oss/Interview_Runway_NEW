Indian-to-US Title Translation Workbook
Workbook for translating Indian titles, company context, and scope into globally legible evidence.
How to use: Replace bracketed fields, delete instructions that do not apply, and keep every claim truthful and interview-defensible.
Translation rule
Preserve the official title in the employment record.
Add a functional explanation only when the title is unfamiliar.
Translate scope with users, systems, decisions, and team boundaries.
Never inflate level or people-management responsibility.
Role translation worksheet
Official title: [____________]
Functional equivalent: [backend / platform / mobile / data / engineering lead]
Scope: [users, traffic, services, regions, team size]
Decision authority: [what you independently owned]
Evidence: [result, metric, incident, launch, or durable mechanism]
Company context sentence
[Company] is a [industry/product] organization serving [customer/scale]. My team owned [system/workflow], and my role covered [functional scope].
Final checks
Would a US recruiter understand the work without knowing the title ladder?
Can every scope claim be defended in an interview?
Are client, vendor, and parent-company relationships clear?
