## Make a referral easy to assess

Worked example - fictional. Names, organizations, dates, amounts, metrics, and outcomes below are invented to demonstrate the method. Do not copy them as your own experience. Use only facts you can explain and support.

Learning objective: Ask someone to evaluate fit before asking them to attach their name to an application.

## Prepare before asking

Read the exact opening and choose two requirements you genuinely meet. Provide the role link or identifier, a current resume, a short fit summary, and accurate location facts. A person should not need to reconstruct your background or search for the vacancy to decide whether they are comfortable helping.

## Worked request - fictional

Hi Dev, I hope you are well. I am considering the backend engineer opening on OrbitDesk's notification team. My work on idempotent delivery and service operations appears aligned with the role's reliability requirements. Would you be comfortable taking a quick look at my resume and telling me whether the fit seems credible? If so, and if you are comfortable referring me, I would appreciate it. No pressure either way. I have included the role identifier and my resume in this message.

## What makes the request respectful

It asks for judgment, gives useful evidence, and makes declining easy. If the contact does not know your work, do not imply a close relationship or ask them to endorse claims they cannot assess. If they agree, send one organized packet and follow their process. Thank them regardless of the hiring outcome.

Practice: compress your fit summary into three sentences: target role, two relevant capabilities, and one evidence-rich result. Remove generic praise and explain any important constraint honestly.
