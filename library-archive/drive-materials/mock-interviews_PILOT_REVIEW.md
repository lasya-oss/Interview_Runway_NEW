## What approval sealed into `mock-int-gen`

1. The five-category matrix and role/level spread.
2. The realism contract: competent but imperfect candidates, neutral probing,
   stable facts, sparse overlaps, and noncommittal closes.
3. Match View: a static, blurred photorealistic interview setting, center
   divider, and dialogue only on the active speaker's side.
4. Three learning uses from one timeline: Watch, Practice, and Coach.
5. The structured private coach map and deliberate-imperfection requirements.
6. The editorial duration contract: recruiter 18-21 minutes, coding 32-38,
   system design 35-40, behavioral 26-31, and hiring-manager 31-36. Meet it by
   writing sufficient dialogue or explicit activity blocks before TTS—never by
   distributing artificial silence after synthesis.

The original skill generated the remaining 28 scripts, but human listening
revealed that its structure checks did not protect spoken cadence. The skill
now adds hard sentence and breath-group ceilings, direct-answer shaping,
sentence-sized Hume utterances, and per-voice native-speed calibration. The
corrected recruiter pilot passes those new text, audio, timestamp, and video
checks. The other 29 require the same rewrite and voice-calibration pass after
this pilot receives human approval.
